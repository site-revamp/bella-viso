# BellaViso Medical Center – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

BellaViso Medical Center is a multidisciplinary healthcare and aesthetic clinic located in Al Barsha 1, Dubai. The clinic provides general and cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, dental implants, dermatology, laser hair removal and medical aesthetic treatments.

The redesigned homepage focuses on clearly presenting the clinic's two main service areas, dental care and aesthetic treatments, while improving treatment discovery and simplifying the appointment enquiry journey.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Brand Positioning and Service Hierarchy

- Establishes a clearer distinction between dental healthcare and dermatology and aesthetic services.
- Highlights the clinic's multidisciplinary offering without allowing individual treatment categories to compete for attention.
- Communicates the clinic's main areas of expertise more effectively.

**Issue addressed:** The original homepage did not sufficiently distinguish between its dental, dermatological and aesthetic services, making the clinic's overall offering less clear.

### 2. Improved Visual Hierarchy and Treatment Discovery

- Gives greater prominence to important treatments such as Hollywood Smile, dental braces, dental implants, HydraFacial and laser hair removal.
- Organises treatments into more structured, easy-to-scan sections.
- Makes key services easier to identify and explore.

**Issue addressed:** Important treatments lacked sufficient visual organisation, making service discovery less intuitive and reducing the visibility of key offerings.

### 3. Clearer Appointment and Contact Journey

- Improves the visibility of appointment and contact actions.
- Presents telephone, email and WhatsApp contact options more consistently.
- Creates a more straightforward pathway from exploring treatments to making an enquiry.

**Issue addressed:** Multiple published contact details and less prominent appointment actions created uncertainty about how visitors should contact the clinic.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Creates a more cohesive medical and aesthetic appearance that reflects both sides of the clinic's services.
- **Layout and Hierarchy:** Separates dental and aesthetic treatment sections to improve content organisation.
- **User Experience:** Makes important treatments easier to scan and navigate.
- **Navigation:** Establishes clearer pathways between treatment categories and relevant services.
- **Contact Accessibility:** Gives greater prominence to appointment enquiries and essential contact information.
- **Overall Appearance:** Balances clinical professionalism with the clinic's aesthetic positioning through a more structured and consistent homepage design.

---

**Project Focus:** Clearer service positioning, improved treatment discovery and a simpler appointment enquiry journey.
